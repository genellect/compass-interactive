# COMPASS Interactive Code of Conduct

COMPASS Interactive is an educational technology project. Project spaces must
support careful technical work, responsible security research, and respectful
collaboration across differences in experience, language, identity, and
background.

## Expected conduct

Participants are expected to:

- communicate with clarity, patience, and respect;
- critique ideas, code, evidence, and outcomes rather than people;
- make room for questions and good-faith disagreement;
- protect student, educator, contributor, and reporter privacy;
- disclose conflicts of interest and relevant third-party rights; and
- follow the repository's security, contribution, and licensing boundaries.

## Unacceptable conduct

The following is not acceptable in project-controlled spaces:

- harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, or personal
  attacks;
- publishing another person's private information or authenticated material;
- pressuring a person to disclose credentials, identity documents, private
  lecture content, or confidential communications;
- knowingly placing exploit details or sensitive evidence in a public channel;
- impersonation, misleading claims of affiliation, or misuse of project marks;
  or
- sustained disruption, spam, or retaliation against a reporter or reviewer.

## Scope

This code applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews,
project-managed communication, and public representation of the project by an
authorized representative. GitHub's terms and platform policies also apply.

## Reporting

Do not place sensitive conduct details, personal data, or evidence in a public
issue. Request a private maintainer contact by opening an issue titled
`Private conduct contact requested` with no incident details. For a suspected
security vulnerability or credential exposure, use the private process in
[`.github/SECURITY.md`](.github/SECURITY.md) instead.

Reports will be handled as discreetly as reasonably possible. Information may
be shared with the minimum people needed to assess safety, preserve evidence,
or comply with law. A knowingly false report may itself violate this code;
good-faith reports do not.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, request a correction, issue a warning,
limit participation, reject a contribution, temporarily restrict contact, or
permanently exclude a participant. The response will be proportionate to
severity, repetition, safety impact, and remediation. Immediate action may be
taken when needed to protect people, data, infrastructure, or the integrity of
the project.
